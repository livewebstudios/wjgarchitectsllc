THE FIRM

Every project gets the same pride. Whatever the size.

WJG Architects was incorporated in 2004. Wayne J. Guskind, AIA, has been a licensed architect since 1989. The practice started in industrial buildings and data floor design. Today it covers homes, restaurants and commercial space too.

Wayne produces many of the drawings himself, and he reviews and approves every sheet that leaves the office.
