HOW WE WORK

What your architect does, from first meeting to final inspection.

  1. 01

    PROGRAM

    We sit down with you and pin down what the project has to do, what it needs, and what limits it.

    • Programming
  2. 02

    SITE, ZONING AND CODE

    We study the lot, run the zoning and the building code, and testify at the zoning board when a variance is needed.

    • Site Analysis
    • Zoning Analysis
    • Planning and Zoning Board Testimony
    • Building Code Analysis
    • Barrier Free Design

    Need a variance?

    If your project needs zoning approval, we represent you at the board meeting and give professional testimony.

    ZONING AND CODES
  3. 03

    DESIGN

    Concept sketches first, so you can see it. Then we refine it and add the technical detail.

    • Schematic Design
    • Design Development
  4. 04

    DOCUMENTS

    Full construction drawings and specifications, coordinated with the mechanical, electrical and plumbing engineers.

    • Construction Documents
    • Technical Specifications
  5. 05

    BUILD

    We help you choose a contractor and manage the bids. Then we visit the site to keep the work true to the drawings.

    • Bidding and Negotiation
    • Construction Administration
    • Field Inspection

THE FIRM

Every project gets the same pride. Whatever the size.

WJG Architects was incorporated in 2004. Wayne J. Guskind, AIA, has been a licensed architect since 1989. The practice started in industrial buildings and data floor design. Today it covers homes, restaurants and commercial space too.

Wayne produces many of the drawings himself, and he reviews and approves every sheet that leaves the office.
